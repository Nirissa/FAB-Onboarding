Business account

Opening an account, start to finish.

You will not fill in a form. FAB Ai reads your licence and your company documents, prepares each step, and waits for your approval. Nothing is submitted until you give it.

Six steps Stop whenever you like. Everything is saved as you go.
1Business structureYour trade licence, then the people and companies behind the business.
2Company detailsThe company's own particulars, read from your documents for you to confirm.
3Tax assessmentYour tax declaration. Short, and required by law.
4Channel managementWho on your team can do what in the bank.
5ProductsChoose the account that suits how the business runs.
6SignatureVerify yourself, sign, and submit.

What to have with you

  • Trade licence. For Dubai and Abu Dhabi, the number on its own is enough.
  • Memorandum and articles of association. A photo or a scan will do.
  • A mobile number and email for each shareholder and signatory. Nobody is contacted until you submit.

After you submit, everyone who has to sign gets a link to verify their face and sign. You can then follow who has signed and who has not. Stop at any point and come back with your mobile number.

Who runs the account

An administrator can do everything on the FAB Business platform, including moving money and creating other users. There is no partial access, so this is a small decision with a large consequence. Up to three.
0 of 3 administrators assigned
From your organisation
Everyone on the record from step 1. Turning someone on grants full access to the platform.
External administrator
Someone who runs the banking but is not an owner or a signatory, for example a finance manager or an accountant.
